# Product: Kiro Deep Dive training site
A free, public, bilingual (English / Traditional Chinese) training site for developers on
spec-driven and test-driven development with Kiro. About two and a half hours, eleven chapters in customer-journey order;
Chapters 2, 7 and 8 cover governance (decide, roll out with MDM, monitor) for security,
endpoint and operations teams.
Audience: developers new to Kiro or to test-first work with AI agents.
Success: a learner can run one change through requirements → red test → green → refactor → gate.
The spec for this site lives in .kiro/specs/kiro-deep-dive-site/.
